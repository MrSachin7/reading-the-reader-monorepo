\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We asked whether the gaze-adaptive reading loop can be cleanly separated into replaceable modules under real-time conditions, and answered with a built and measured platform: a two-screen, researcher-operated system whose four modules sit behind build-checked contracts, driven by a real 90\,Hz eye tracker with single-host round trips within a 100\,ms budget, analysed live by a collaborating study's pipeline attached through its provider contract, and recorded into replayable sessions, with every result in this paper recomputed from the exported records.
Its self-measurement exposed, and its revision geometrically removed, an over-repositioning defect in our own context-preserving restore, and the same records exposed a duplicate-recording defect in provider event extraction, since fixed, which is precisely the kind of finding an instrument should produce about itself.
