\caption{The Reading the Reader platform as a researcher--participant adaptive loop. The eye tracker is the sensing interface between the participant and the platform; the backend adapts the participant's text and mirrors the gaze and the derived analysis (fixations, saccades, regressions) to the researcher console, where the session is steered. An external decision provider may propose interventions through a defined API, which the researcher approves in advisory mode or which apply directly in autonomous mode.}
\label{fig:design-platform-master}
