\chapter{Key Concepts and Related Work}
\label{ch:key-concepts-related-work}

This chapter establishes the conceptual and scholarly foundation for the thesis.
Sec.~\ref{sec:key-concepts} defines the domain terms that the rest of the thesis builds on;
Sec.~\ref{sec:related-work} surveys the academic work most directly relevant to adaptive reading and gaze-based intervention;
Sec.~\ref{sec:market-landscape} surveys the current tool and product landscape;
Sec.~\ref{sec:gaps-opportunities} identifies the gaps that motivate the thesis contribution;
Sec.~\ref{sec:final-problem-statement} states the refined problem and measurable research questions;
and Sec.~\ref{sec:ch2-summary} summarises the chapter.
