This chapter reports what we measured.
It follows the evaluation strategy of \cref{sec:evaluation-strategy}, which maps each research question to a method of assessment (\cref{tab:rq-evaluation}), and it presents the evidence without interpreting it.
The comparison with prior work, the reading of the results against the problem statement, and the limitations they imply are deferred to \cref{ch:discussion}.
We state results as measurements, distributions, and demonstrations; we do not argue from them here.
