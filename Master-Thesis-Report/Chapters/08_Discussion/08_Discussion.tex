\Cref{ch:evaluation} reported what the platform measured, but it did not interpret those measurements.
This chapter provides that interpretation.
We read the results against the problem statement of \cref{sec:final-problem-statement}, compare them with the prior work of \cref{ch:key-concepts-related-work}, and set out the limitations they imply.
The chapter adds no new results.
Taken together, the evidence gives a clear verdict.
The two architectural goals, modularity and researcher control, are met with strong evidence.
The two runtime goals, the sensing pipeline and the context-preserving intervention, are met for the paths that the recorded sessions used, and each one reveals a single honest finding that we carry into the future work.
