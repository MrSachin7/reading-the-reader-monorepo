## Purpose

This page records the architectural decisions made during development — the *why* behind each major choice. These are the arguments the thesis defence will examine.

A decision record answers:
1. What was the decision?
2. What alternatives were considered?
3. What drove the choice?
4. What are the trade-offs accepted?
