## What This Page Will Cover

The decision layer is the architectural centrepiece of the thesis: the seam that makes decision strategies interchangeable. It receives sensing events and produces intervention requests without knowing how the intervention will be applied.

### Topics

- The decision strategy abstraction: what the interface looks like and what it guarantees
- Built-in strategies: manual (researcher-triggered), rule-based (threshold-based), hybrid
- External provider mode: how a third party plugs in via `/ws/provider` without gaining session authority
- Strategy lifecycle: how strategies are enabled, disabled, and switched per session
- Logging: how the origin and rationale of each intervention decision is recorded
