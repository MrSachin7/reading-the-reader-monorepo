## What This Page Will Cover

The intervention layer receives a decision and applies it to the reading UI. Its primary responsibility is ensuring that adaptations are applied correctly — at the right moment, with reading position preserved.

### Topics

- Timing modes: Immediate, SentenceEnd, ParagraphEnd, PageTurn — when each is appropriate
- Layout commit policy: how the viewport adjusts to keep the anchor point visible
- Context preservation mechanics: word/line anchoring before and after layout change
- Cooldown and guardrails: preventing intervention storms
- Intervention event logging: timestamp, source, type, and outcome

### Relation to Requirements

- FR7 — Intervention Runtime (pluggable modules, declarative inputs)
- FR9 — Context Preservation (anchor, viewport adjustment, failure logging)
- NFR6 — Extensibility (additive only)
