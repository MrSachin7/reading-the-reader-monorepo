## What This Page Will Cover

Results from the user studies and validation experiments measuring:

- **Usability** — SUS scores for the researcher-facing workflow
- **Reading flow** — fixation duration, regression rate, and reading speed during interventions vs. control
- **Perceived disruption** — self-reported disruption ratings after sessions with interventions
- **Context preservation** — rate of reading position loss during layout-changing interventions
- **Latency** — measured end-to-end latency from gaze signal to UI commit
