# Reading the Reader

**An adaptive reading platform built as an MSc thesis at DTU Compute.**

The system connects to Tobii eye trackers, runs controlled reading sessions, mirrors the participant view to a researcher screen in real time, and applies context-preserving micro-interventions — all through a modular architecture designed so future teams can swap decision strategies and intervention modules without touching the core.
