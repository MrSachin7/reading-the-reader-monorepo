# Limitations

Stated plainly, because the contribution is clearer when its boundaries are explicit (full treatment in thesis Chapter 8):

- **Scope limits fixed at the start.** Reading material is Markdown only (reflowable text, no PDF). The recorded sessions used a small convenience sample, not the struggling or clinical readers adaptive reading ultimately serves. The thesis makes **no efficacy claim**: it shows the platform can apply interventions and measure their immediate cost, not that any intervention improves reading.
- **AI support is architectural, not built.** The contract and the external-provider path exist and were exercised end to end on real hardware, but with a fixed-rule reference provider; no learned decision model was implemented or run.
- **The integration contract is validated mostly by its authors.** One independently designed pipeline was absorbed unmodified (with a connector written by the platform authors); no outside team has yet implemented the protocol from the documentation alone.
- **The revised context restore is verified geometrically, not behaviourally.** The deterministic sweep confirms the over-repositioning is gone; a re-test with readers is pending. Line-width changes repaginate and are not compensated.
- **The frontend has no automated test suite.** Browser-side flows were verified by typed builds, manual walkthrough, and the committed sweep harness; the backend carries the contract tests (100 of 100 passing).
- **Single-operator deployment.** One session at a time, file-backed storage, single host by design (DR7); a concurrent multi-session deployment would need to revisit persistence and session keying.
