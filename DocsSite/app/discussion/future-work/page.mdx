## What This Page Will Cover

The natural continuation of this work for future Reading the Reader teams. This section discusses what remains open — not as failures of this thesis, but as the designed extension points.

### Planned Topics

**Architecture extensions**
- Replacing file-backed persistence with a real database for multi-session research programmes
- Privacy and access control hardening for use with external participants
- Export provenance: stamping sessions with build version and full configuration

**Decision strategy extensions**
- Implementing a real AI-based decision provider against the existing contract
- Evaluating reinforcement learning approaches for adaptive triggering
- Multi-modal sensing: adding input beyond gaze (keypress latency, scroll behaviour)

**Intervention extensions**
- New intervention types beyond typography (audio cues, pace suggestions)
- Evaluating longer-form interventions across full reading sessions
- Adaptive strategy selection based on reader history

**Research extensions**
- Longitudinal studies with repeat participants
- Comparison of automated vs. human-in-the-loop intervention quality
- Domain-specific reading (technical texts, academic papers)
